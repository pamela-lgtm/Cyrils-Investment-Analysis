Infrastructure Outlook

Three significant roadway projects are planned or underway in the immediate area — meaningful tailwinds for an appreciation thesis, with near-term construction as the trade-off:

SR 534 — the "Narcoossee Reliever" (CFX)
A planned 14-mile limited-access toll road from SR 417 near Boggy Creek Rd to Nova Rd, explicitly designed to improve airport connectivity and relieve congestion on Narcoossee, Boggy Creek, and Cyrils Drive. Currently in design.
Sunbridge Parkway Extension (Osceola County)
A ~$255M, 15–20-mile four-lane road adding a second north-south connection into St. Cloud, serving the 27,000-acre Sunbridge community (7,000+ planned homes) — which includes Weslyn Park — and Harmony West.
Cyrils Drive Widening (underway)
The subject street is being widened from two to four lanes between Narcoossee and Absher Rd, with sidewalks and bike accommodations. Construction began spring 2024; the first three phases within Sunbridge are complete.

Investment Read — Appreciation Lens

Weighed against Pete's goal of appreciation with an all-cash purchase, the picture is more favorable than a yield-first read would suggest. With no mortgage, the heavy carrying costs are a manageable expense rather than a return-killer, and he can hold comfortably through the build-out.

Working in Pete's Favor

  • Entry ~$100K below the April 2024 sale price ($399,900 → ~$300K) — buying near a cyclical low.
  • Early position in a Tavistock master-planned community (the Lake Nona developer) before full build-out and amenity maturity.
  • Three roadway catalysts — SR 534, Sunbridge Parkway, Cyrils widening — classic appreciation drivers.
  • All-cash means no interest drag while waiting; he can hold patiently.
  • 2023 build + Flood Zone X — low capex risk, no flood-insurance drag.
  • Strong airport / Medical City proximity underpins rental demand during the hold.

Cautions for Appreciation

  • In an active build-out, resale competes with new builder inventory and incentives.
  • Non-homestead taxes likely reassess upward, raising carry.
  • Property is pending — backup-offer only right now.
  • SR 534's exact routing near the community should be confirmed.

Bottom Line

For an all-cash buyer playing appreciation, this is a genuine win. Pete is acquiring a 2023-built home in a Tavistock growth community for under his $300,000 budget — and at that price, in this area, that alone is the headline. The seller bought closer to the peak and, squeezed by builder rate buydowns and severe new-construction competition, simply couldn't sell and now wants out; their loss becomes Pete's entry advantage. He gets an early, below-peak position with real infrastructure tailwinds, and a rent that fully carries the property while it appreciates. Even the CDD works in his favor — it funds the resort-style amenities (pool, clubhouse, parks, and trails) that renters actively seek, supporting both occupancy and rent. The one practical move: it's pending, so a backup offer is the play.
